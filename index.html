Day1

Tuesday, August 18, 2026

Calgary Arrival → Canmore

125 km · ~1.5 hr · Return rental YYC → YYC

  • Fly into Calgary YYC Morning

    Collect luggage and pick up the SUV rental at YYC. This is now a round-trip rental, so confirm the Calgary return location and keep the reservation simple.

  • Calgary grocery stop ~30 min

    Stock snacks, breakfast items, water, sunscreen, and trail food before heading west. Grocery options are easy in Canmore, but Calgary is the lowest-friction first stop.

  • Drive to Canmore Afternoon

    Take Hwy 2 north, then Trans-Canada Hwy 1 west. The first big mountain reveal comes as the Bow Valley opens near Canmore.

  • Check into Canmore Airbnb Evening

    Settle into the first base. Walk Main Street, confirm shuttle and Pursuit reservations, and keep the first night easy.

Confirmed Pursuit Reservations Open Top is Aug 20 at 12:00 PM, Banff Gondola is Aug 20 from 5:30 PM to 7:30 PM, Columbia Icefield is Aug 23 at 1:00 PM with 12:45 PM boarding, and Maligne Lake Cruise is Aug 25 at 11:45 AM with 11:30 AM boarding. Skywalk and Golden Skybridge do not require date/time selection.
Day2

Wednesday, August 19, 2026

Lake Louise + Moraine Lake

Canmore base · shuttle day · 2–3 hr total driving

  • Lake Louise Park & Ride Early morning

    Drive to the Lake Louise Ski Resort Park & Ride and check in for your reserved Parks Canada shuttle. Use the connector to see both Lake Louise and Moraine Lake in one day.

  • Moraine Lake — Rockpile viewpoint Morning

    Start with the Valley of Ten Peaks while light is clean and crowds are lower. Rockpile is the essential short walk; canoe rentals are optional if the line is reasonable.

  • Lake Louise lakeshore or Tea House hike Midday

    For a lighter day, walk the lakeshore to Victoria Glacier views and keep time for a proper lunch. For stronger hikers, Lake Agnes Tea House is the best upgrade.

  • Return to Canmore without adding another major stop Late afternoon

    Protect this as the biggest lake day of the Banff section. Save Johnston Canyon for tomorrow, when it can be done early and unrushed.

Moraine Lake Logistics Personal vehicles are not permitted on Moraine Lake Road. Use Parks Canada shuttle, Roam transit, or a licensed commercial operator; reserve early and set an alarm for released seats.
Day3

Thursday, August 20, 2026

Johnston Canyon + Banff Viewpoints

Local Banff loop · 2–3 hr total driving

  • Johnston Canyon Lower + Upper Falls Early morning

    Go early while parking and catwalks are calmer. This is the best easy waterfall walk in the Banff section and fits better here than after the Lake Louise shuttle day.

  • Banff Avenue + quick lunch Late morning

    Use Banff town as the reset between Johnston Canyon and the timed Pursuit activities. Keep lunch simple so the noon tour is not rushed.

  • Open Top Touring 12:00 PM · Pursuit Pass

    Arrive at the Brewster Transportation Centre with a small buffer. This is the only required Open Top time; the tour anchors the middle of the Banff day.

  • Bow Falls + Banff viewpoints Afternoon buffer

    Use the gap before the gondola for Bow Falls, Surprise Corner, or a quiet coffee break. Do not add Lake Minnewanka unless the group has more energy than expected.

  • Banff Gondola — Sulphur Mountain 5:30 PM up · 7:30 PM down

    Ride to the summit boardwalk for the cleanest panoramic view of the Bow Valley. The two-hour window gives enough time for Sanson's Peak, photos, and an unhurried descent.

  • Pack for Yoho / Golden Evening

    Tomorrow crosses the Continental Divide. Confirm Golden Airbnb check-in and make sure fuel, snacks, and offline maps are set.

Day4

Friday, August 21, 2026

Canmore → Golden

130 km · 1.5–2.5 hr + Yoho stops

  • Scenic drive over Kicking Horse Pass Morning

    Leave Canmore after breakfast and head west past Lake Louise into Yoho. The drive is short enough to make the transfer day feel like sightseeing, not a slog.

  • Spiral Tunnels viewpoint Quick stop

    Classic railway engineering stop on the Trans-Canada. Worth a short pullout if visibility is good and timing is easy.

  • Takakkaw Falls Midday · seasonal road

    Make this the transfer-day anchor while you are already passing through Yoho. The road is seasonal and winding, so skip it if conditions or timing are poor.

  • Natural Bridge + Emerald Lake preview Afternoon

    Natural Bridge is a fast, high-value stop. If the group still has energy, use Emerald Lake as a first look and save the slower shoreline time for tomorrow.

  • Check into Golden Airbnb Evening

    Golden gives you the right mix of lodging, food options, and access to Yoho. Keep dinner local and avoid adding another long detour.

Day5

Saturday, August 22, 2026

Yoho National Park

Golden base · 2–3 hr total driving

  • Emerald Lake slow morning Morning

    Walk part of the shoreline before the day fills in, rent canoes if conditions are calm, and treat this as the Yoho anchor rather than a quick photo stop.

  • Natural Bridge or Iceline trail segment Midday

    Keep the default comfortable with Natural Bridge, lunch, and short walks. If the group wants a real hiking day, replace the easier stops with a planned Iceline segment.

  • Wapta Falls + optional Golden Skybridge Afternoon

    Wapta Falls is the nature-forward pick and deserves priority. Golden Skybridge is the paid attraction option if the group wants something structured before dinner.

  • Early night before the northbound transfer Evening

    Tomorrow is a long scenic drive to Valemount. Fuel up, pack lunch, and keep the Icefields Parkway stops selective.

Why Golden Works Golden is the efficient Yoho base: it keeps Emerald Lake, Natural Bridge, Takakkaw Falls, Wapta Falls, and dinner options close without forcing another Banff hotel move.
Day6

Sunday, August 23, 2026

Golden → Valemount

Icefields Parkway · 1:00 PM Icefield reservation · long road day

  • Leave Golden early Around 7:00 AM

    This is the most timing-sensitive transfer. Drive back through Lake Louise, turn north onto the Icefields Parkway, and protect the Columbia Icefield check-in buffer.

  • Bow Lake + Peyto Lake viewpoints Quick stops only

    Use these as high-value, low-time stops before the reservation. Keep each stop short and skip any hike that threatens the 12:45 PM Icefield boarding time.

  • Columbia Icefield Adventure + Skywalk 1:00 PM · board 12:45 PM

    This is the fixed point of the day. Allow 2.5 to 3 hours for the combined experience and assume the Skywalk follows without a separate date or time selection.

  • Athabasca Falls if timing is still good Late afternoon

    Make this the only likely post-Icefield sightseeing stop. If the day is running late, continue through Jasper instead and save energy for the Valemount arrival.

  • Jasper supply stop, then Valemount Airbnb Evening

    Pick up groceries or dinner in Jasper before continuing west on Hwy 16. Expect a later arrival because the Icefield reservation now sits on this road day.

Valemount Base Reality Check Valemount is a workable Jasper access base, but not a Jasper town stay. Budget about 1.5 hours each way to Jasper before adding Maligne Lake, Pyramid Lake, or town activities.
Days7–8

Monday–Tuesday, August 24–25, 2026

Mount Robson + One Strong Jasper Day

Valemount base · one local day, one Jasper day

Aug 24 — Mount Robson / Valemount

  • Mount Robson viewpoint + visitor area Morning

    Use the local advantage of Valemount instead of driving back to Jasper immediately. If the peak is clear, this is one of the most memorable mountain views of the trip.

  • Kinney Lake trail or easier local recovery Half day

    For active travelers, hike the lower Berg Lake Trail toward Kinney Lake as conditions allow. For a softer day, keep it to Robson viewpoints, Valemount food, laundry, and recovery.

  • Prepare for Jasper day Evening

    Pack food, layers, and fuel for the fixed Maligne Lake Cruise reservation tomorrow. The goal is to make the longer out-and-back feel intentional.

Aug 25 — Jasper / Maligne Road

  • Medicine Lake viewpoint Morning

    Leave Valemount early and drive through Jasper to Maligne Road. Medicine Lake is the best low-effort stop before continuing to Maligne Lake.

  • Maligne Lake Cruise 11:45 AM · board 11:30 AM

    Use this as the Jasper anchor. Leave Valemount early enough to reach Maligne Lake with parking and wildlife-delay buffer before the 11:30 AM boarding time.

  • Pyramid Lake + Jasper town dinner Late day

    If timing allows, add Pyramid Island before dinner in Jasper. Skip extra attractions if the group is tired; the Valemount return still adds about 1.5 hours.

Northern Rockies Base Valemount keeps Mount Robson close and gives the group a dedicated Jasper day for Maligne Lake, Pyramid Lake, and town time.
Day9

Wednesday, August 26, 2026

Valemount → Calgary

~530 km · long road day · no flight pressure

  • Checkout from Valemount By 10am

    Make this the long travel day instead of departure day. Check DriveBC and 511 Alberta before leaving, then head east through Mount Robson and Jasper.

  • Drive the Parkway without another timed attraction Flexible stops

    Columbia Icefield is already reserved for Aug 23, so keep this return day simpler. Use any stops as weather-and-energy choices, not obligations.

  • Check into Calgary hotel Evening

    Stay in Calgary with easy access to YYC tomorrow. This removes the stressful mountain drive from flight day and makes the rental return straightforward.

Why This Works Better Moving Columbia Icefield to Aug 23 makes Aug 26 a cleaner return to Calgary. The long drive still exists, but there is no fixed attraction time or flight timing behind it.
Day10

Thursday, August 27, 2026

Calgary → Fly Out

10–25 min local transfer · Return SUV at YYC

  • Slow morning near YYC Flight dependent

    Breakfast, final packing, and a short local drive. No mountain highway pressure on departure day.

  • Return SUV at Calgary YYC Per flight schedule

    Allow the normal airport buffer for rental return, bag drop, and security. The route is now simple enough for morning, midday, or evening flights.

Departure Day This is now intentionally boring: short drive, rental return, airport. That is the point.

Quick Reference

Highlights + Planning Reference

Moraine Lake

Valley of Ten Peaks, accessed by shuttle or licensed operator. This is the most important reservation in the Banff portion.

Lake Louise

Pair the lakeshore with Moraine Lake, or upgrade to Lake Agnes Tea House if the group wants a moderate hike.

Emerald Lake

The Yoho anchor. Less urban than Banff, easier to linger, and a strong reason to keep the Golden base.

Takakkaw Falls

High-impact waterfall stop in Yoho. Best as a transfer-day anchor if the seasonal road is open.

Columbia Icefield

Reserved for Aug 23 at 1:00 PM on the Golden-to-Valemount transfer. Keep the morning viewpoint stops short and the afternoon flexible.

Maligne Lake + Mount Robson

Treat Maligne as one intentional Jasper day and Mount Robson as the local Valemount payoff. That keeps the northern base worthwhile.

Park Admission

Your dates fall inside Parks Canada's 2026 free-admission window, but shuttle tickets, paid parking, tours, and attraction reservations still need to be booked separately.

Jasper Closures

Do not build the plan around Maligne Canyon or Edith Cavell for 2026. Keep Jasper flexible and confirm road/trail status close to travel.

August Weather + Packing

Expect warm afternoons, cool mornings/evenings, and fast mountain weather changes. Pack T-shirts, long pants, fleece or light puffer, rain shell with hood, trail shoes, sunglasses, sunscreen, bug spray, and a small daypack. Bring a warm hat or light gloves for Columbia Icefield, Banff Gondola, Moraine/Louise mornings, and Maligne Lake.

Driving + Fuel Notes

Top off in Canmore, Golden, Jasper, and Valemount. Keep offline maps for Yoho, Icefields Parkway, Hwy 16, Maligne Road, and the Aug 26 Valemount-to-Calgary return.
