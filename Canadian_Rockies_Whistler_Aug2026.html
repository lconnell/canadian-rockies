Arrive Vancouver → Sea to Sky → Whistler

▸ Hwy 99 between Squamish and Whistler is breathtaking but has no services — fill the tank before you leave Vancouver. The drive is only 2 hours but you'll want to stop for photos.

Monday, Aug 19
Whistler — Peak 2 Peak & The Village
★ Gondola▸ Walk
BASE: Whistler, BC
  • 8:00 amBreakfast — Purebread (best bakery in Whistler, lineup worth it), Tractor Everyday Food, or your hotel. Fuel up.
  • 9:00 amWhistler Gondola — ride up to Whistler Mountain (Roundhouse Lodge, 1,850m). Views of the glacier, peaks, and valley open up as you ascend. Walk around the Roundhouse area — grab a coffee with a view.
  • 10:00 amPeak 2 Peak Gondola Don't Miss — the signature Whistler experience. A 4.4 km cable car connects Whistler Mountain to Blackcomb Mountain, spanning a world-record 3 km open span 436 m above the valley floor. The views of glaciers, alpine meadows, and the valley are extraordinary. Allow 45–60 min for the crossing and back (or stay on Blackcomb).
  • 11:00 amBlackcomb Mountain summit — Horstman Glacier viewpoint is an easy 10-min walk from the top station. The glacier sits at 2,440m and is still skiable in summer. The alpine meadow views are as good as anything you'll see in the Rockies.
  • 12:30 pmRide gondola back down to Whistler Village. Lunch — 21 Steps Kitchen (rooftop patio) or La Cantina (tacos in the village). Pick up trail snacks at a grocery for tomorrow's long drive.
  • 2:00 pmValley Trail — the 40 km paved trail network connects Whistler's neighbourhoods through forest and along Lost Lake. Walk or rent bikes (15–30 min is enough for a taste). Flat, easy, beautiful.
  • 3:30 pmOptional: Audain Art Museum (45 min) — world-class collection of Northwest Coast Indigenous art and BC landscape painting. One of the best small art museums in Canada.
  • 6:30 pmDinner — Alta Bistro (farm-to-table, excellent), Peaked Pies (Australian meat pies, casual), or back to Araxi if you didn't go last night.
  • Before bedSet your alarm for 6:50 am tomorrow Critical Action — Moraine Lake shuttles for Aug 22 open on Aug 20 at 7:00 am PDT (= 8:00 am MDT). You must book the moment slots open at reservation.pc.gc.ca. Target the 9:30 am departure from Lake Louise Park & Ride. Do not miss this window.
▸ Peak 2 Peak tickets: book in advance at whistlerblackcomb.com (the gondola combo is around CA$85/person in summer). Morning is best — cloud can roll in by afternoon.

Wednesday, Aug 21
Revelstoke → Golden Skybridge → Yoho → Canmore
★ Activity▸ Walk▸ Drive
MOVE TO: Canmore, AB  ·  Drive: ~4.5 hrs moving, stops add ~3 hrs
  • 8:00 amBreakfast in Revelstoke — Conversations Coffee or Dose. Check out.
  • 8:30 amDrive east on Trans-Canada (Hwy 1) through Glacier National Park — Rogers Pass. Dramatic mountain scenery as the highway crests the pass at 1,330m. No need to stop (save time), but the drive through the pass is itself excellent. ~1.5 hrs to Golden.
  • 10:00 amGolden Skybridge Pursuit Pass — two suspension bridges spanning a deep canyon above the Columbia River with Rocky Mountain views. The longer bridge is among the highest in North America. The shorter bridge has a zip line option if anyone is feeling brave. Allow 90 min. Book your time slot at goldenbc.ca/skybridge.
  • 11:30 amContinue east on Trans-Canada into Yoho National Park (Field, BC). You've crossed into the Alberta Rockies geological zone — the peaks change character immediately.
  • 12:00 pmSpiral Tunnels viewpoint — quick pull-off above Field on the Trans-Canada. The CPR railway loops through the mountains in a double figure-eight spiral to safely descend the grade. If a freight train is actively passing through, it's extraordinary engineering in dramatic scenery. 10 min stop.
  • 12:15 pmNatural Bridge — 10-min stop. The Kicking Horse River has carved through solid quartzite, forming a natural arch with brilliant glacial green water rushing beneath. Just off the road near the Emerald Lake junction.
  • 12:30 pmEmerald Lake — the most intensely coloured lake in Yoho. A deep tropical green ringed by forest and the President Range. Lunch at Emerald Lake Lodge restaurant (dining room, or grab from the café) or your own picnic. Short lakeshore walk (30 min, flat). Canoe rentals if anyone wants to paddle.
  • 2:30 pmContinue east on Trans-Canada through Lake Louise and east to Canmore. 2 hrs.
  • 4:30 pmArrive Canmore. Check in — three nights here. Drop bags, walk downtown along the creek.
  • 6:30 pmDinner — Crazyweed Kitchen or Rocky Mountain Flatbread.
▸ Yoho is a perfect drive-through park — the best stops are right off the road or a few minutes' walk. Don't try to do Takakkaw Falls today (Yoho Valley Road adds 30 min each way) — save your energy for tomorrow.

Friday, Aug 23
Banff — Johnston Canyon & Gondola
▸ Walk★ Gondola
BASE: Canmore, AB
  • 8:00 amBreakfast at Airbnb. Drive via Bow Valley Parkway (Hwy 1A) — the quiet, scenic alternative to the Trans-Canada. Slower, forested, better for wildlife sightings.
  • 9:30 amJohnston Canyon — one of Banff's most accessible and dramatic walks. The paved canyon trail clings to the cliff face on metal catwalk suspended above the rushing Kicking Horse River. Lower Falls (1.1 km, 20 min, easy) — a turquoise curtain of water in a narrow slot canyon. Upper Falls (2.7 km from trailhead, 30 min more) — taller and wilder. Both together take about 1.5 hrs return. Do both.
  • 11:15 amDrive to Banff townsite (20 min).
  • 12:00 pmLunch in Banff — Wild Flour Bakery (sandwiches, excellent), Balkan Restaurant (Greek, casual), or Juniper Bistro (patio with mountain views).
  • 1:30 pmBanff Gondola Pursuit Pass — 8-min gondola to the summit of Sulphur Mountain (2,281m). Walk the Skywalk boardwalk along the ridge (1.6 km, flat, 360° panorama). On a clear day you can see seven mountain ranges. Take the gondola back down. Allow 1.5 hrs.
  • 3:30 pmVermilion Lakes — a 10-min drive west of Banff on Hwy 1A. Three shallow lakes reflecting Mt Rundle and the Banff townsite skyline. Classic mountain photography. Short flat walk or simply drive the road.
  • 4:30 pmDrive back to Canmore (20 min). Last night here — pack bags tonight for the Jasper move tomorrow.
  • 6:30 pmDinner — Brazen (steakhouse, excellent) or Rocky Mountain Flatbread (wood-fired, family-friendly).
▸ Banff Gondola is included in the Pursuit Pass — no separate ticket needed. The Sulphur Mountain summit is cold even in August; bring a layer. Johnston Canyon is one of the few walks the entire crew will love regardless of fitness level.

Interpretive markers show the ice retreat decade by decade. No hike required — all the drama is right there.

  • 3:00 pmPyramid Lake — 7 km north of Jasper on Pyramid Lake Road. Walk the causeway to Pyramid Island (flat, 15 min), a small island connected to the lakeshore by a wooden bridge. The lake perfectly reflects Pyramid Mountain. Canoe and kayak rentals available at the resort dock.
  • 4:30 pmOptional: Miette Hot Springs — the hottest natural hot springs in the Canadian Rockies (54°C at source, cooled for the pools). 63 km round trip from Jasper. Perfect if anyone wants to soak after the cruise day. Bring a bathing suit.
  • 7:00 pmFinal Jasper dinner — Fiddle River Restaurant (seafood and game, excellent) or Something Else Restaurant (Greek-Canadian, local favourite). Pack bags tonight for checkout tomorrow.
▸ Maligne Lake cruise is the best single Jasper activity for a group that wants dramatic scenery without a long hike. The Spirit Island view is worth it. Book the cruise in advance — August slots fill up. If you book Miette Hot Springs, note it closes at 9pm in summer.

Good to Know

The Flow of This Trip

You arrive on BC's coast and the scenery builds progressively: rainforest and granite cliffs (Sea to Sky), resort mountains (Whistler), dry interior valleys (BC interior), then Columbia Mountain passes (Revelstoke, Rogers Pass), and finally the full Rocky Mountain grandeur from Banff to Jasper. It escalates. By the time you reach Moraine Lake and the Icefields Parkway, the buildup pays off.

One-Way Rental Advice

One-way fees from Vancouver YVR to Calgary YYC vary widely by company and how far in advance you book. Call Enterprise, National, and Hertz directly — they sometimes have better one-way rates over the phone than online. Confirm the exact drop-off location at YYC, as off-airport lots require a shuttle. Budget 30 extra minutes at the Calgary end.

Whistler in August

August is peak hiking and biking season but the gondola and Peak 2 Peak are the headline activities for sightseeing visitors. Afternoon cloud is common — do the summit activities in the morning. Whistler Village is pedestrian-only and extremely walkable. Parking is at the Day Lots at the base of both mountains. Your hotel may have shuttle service from the village.

Crossing into Alberta

You enter Alberta on Day 4 via the Trans-Canada near Field, BC (Yoho NP). The landscape changes noticeably — the peaks become more dramatic and the rock layers exposed in the thrust fault geology are extraordinary. If your Parks Canada Discovery Pass isn't purchased before the trip, you can buy it at any park gate. Keep it in the windshield for all subsequent park entry.

Driving the Icefields Parkway

You drive the Parkway in both directions: northbound on Day 7 (Bow Lake, Peyto Lake), southbound on Day 9 (Sunwapta Falls, Icefield Adventure, Skywalk). Both directions have different angles and lighting. Gas up in Lake Louise before going north and in Jasper before heading south — there are no gas stations on the Parkway itself.

What to Pack

Layers every day — mornings are cold even in August (0–8°C at altitude). Rain shell essential (Whistler gets coastal weather). Sunscreen and sunglasses (UV is intense at elevation). Good walking shoes with grip (some paths are rocky). Bathing suit for Miette Hot Springs. Binoculars help for glacier and wildlife viewing.

Wildlife

Bears (black and grizzly), elk, moose, bighorn sheep, and mountain goats are genuinely common across this route. Pull over safely if you see a wildlife jam on the highway — never stop in the lane. Keep food secured in your vehicle at all times, especially overnight in Jasper and Whistler. Bear spray is available at any outdoor store in Canmore or Jasper.

Maligne Lake Cruise Tip

The Spirit Island view is accessible only by the 90-min boat cruise — there is no trail to Spirit Island. The landscape on the water is extraordinary on calm mornings when the lake mirrors the peaks. Bring a warm layer; the lake is at 1,700m and the boat deck is exposed. If weather is uncertain on your planned day, verify cancellation policies when booking.
