Detailed Itinerary

Days 1-4
1
Aug 18Arrive Calgary → Bow Valley
Sleep
Canmore or Dead Man's Flats
Drive
YYC to base: about 1-1.5 hr
  • Pick up one-way SUV at Calgary YYC.
  • At 8:00 am MDT, book the Parks Canada Moraine/Lake Louise shuttle for Aug 20 if not already secured.
  • Buy groceries, breakfast supplies, snacks, and water.
  • Check in and keep the first evening easy.
The shuttle booking is time-critical: remaining seats release two days before departure at 8:00 am Mountain Time. Canmore is more convenient for restaurants and walking; Dead Man's Flats works if the Airbnb is better.
2
Aug 19Banff + Johnston Canyon
Sleep
Canmore or Dead Man's Flats
Anchor
Pursuit Pass: Banff Gondola
  • Start early at Johnston Canyon; do Lower and Upper Falls if the group wants the full walk.
  • Lunch and short browse in Banff town.
  • Ride Banff Gondola with a reserved timed ticket.
  • Add Lake Minnewanka, Cascade Ponds, or Vermilion Lakes if time allows.
Do not leave the gondola to chance; reserve the time before the trip.
3
Aug 20Moraine Lake + Lake Louise
Sleep
Canmore or Dead Man's Flats
Anchor
Parks Canada shuttle
  • Drive to Lake Louise Park & Ride at the ski resort.
  • Use the Parks Canada shuttle for Moraine Lake; start with the Rockpile.
  • Use the connector shuttle for Lake Louise and walk the lakeshore.
  • Return to the Bow Valley and pack for Golden.
Booking rule: for this Aug 20 departure, remaining Parks Canada shuttle seats open Aug 18 at 8:00 am MDT. Be logged in before 8:00 and book immediately.
4
Aug 21Yoho → Golden
Sleep
Golden
Anchor
Pursuit Pass: Golden Skybridge
  • Check out and drive west through Lake Louise into Yoho.
  • Stop at Natural Bridge and Emerald Lake.
  • Continue to Golden for the Golden Skybridge.
  • Dinner in Golden; pack for the Icefields Parkway.
Golden is the right overnight because it avoids backtracking and sets up the Jasper move.

Detailed Itinerary

Days 5-8
5
Aug 22Golden → Icefields → Jasper
Sleep
Jasper area
Anchor
Pursuit Pass: Icefield Adventure + Skywalk
  • Leave Golden early and drive east to Lake Louise, then north on Icefields Parkway.
  • Stop at Bow Lake and Peyto Lake if timing and parking work.
  • Do the Columbia Icefield Adventure vehicle at the reserved time.
  • Do the Columbia Icefield Skywalk, then continue to Jasper.
Book the vehicle departure time first; build the day around that reservation.
6
Aug 23Maligne Lake + Jasper
Sleep
Jasper area
Anchor
Pursuit Pass: Spirit Island Cruise
  • Drive Maligne Road to Maligne Lake.
  • Take the Maligne Lake Spirit Island Cruise; morning is preferred.
  • Add Pyramid Lake, Pyramid Island, Lake Annette, or easy Jasper town time.
  • Final Jasper dinner and repack for the Kamloops transfer.
Keep this day protected. The cruise is the Jasper anchor, not a casual add-on.
7
Aug 24Jasper → Kamloops
Sleep
Kamloops
Purpose
Break the long BC transfer
  • Check out of Jasper and start west.
  • Use the day for distance, meals, fuel, and driver breaks.
  • Mount Robson is skipped as a planned activity.
  • Use Kamloops for a normal hotel, dinner, and rest.
This is intentionally practical. It keeps Jasper to Whistler from becoming an exhausting push.
8
Aug 25Kamloops → Whistler
Sleep
Whistler
Route
Lillooet + Duffey Lake Road
  • Drive Kamloops to Cache Creek / Lillooet.
  • Lunch in Lillooet or Pemberton depending on pace.
  • Continue over Duffey Lake Road to Whistler.
  • Check in, walk the village, and confirm any Peak 2 Peak timing.
Fuel conservatively and check DriveBC before committing to the Duffey route.

Vancouver
Route
Sea-to-Sky Highway
  • If weather is good and tickets work, do Peak 2 Peak early.

Watch itemsRoad, Smoke, Access
  • Check Parks Canada updates before Banff, Yoho, and Jasper days.
  • Check DriveBC before Kamloops to Whistler and Sea-to-Sky.

  • Do not add Jasper hikes unless access and group energy are both confirmed.
